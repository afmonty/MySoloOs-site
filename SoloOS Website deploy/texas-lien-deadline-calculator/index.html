Your deadline

Pick a project type and a month to see your notice deadline.

How this is calculated. Texas Property Code §53.056 requires a claimant who is not the original contractor to send a notice of claim to the owner and the original contractor. For projects other than residential construction projects, it is due no later than the 15th day of the third month after the month the labor or materials were provided. For residential construction projects it is the 15th day of the second month. §53.003(e) extends a deadline that falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to the next day that is not one.

This is not legal advice and is not a substitute for an attorney. It covers private, non-bonded projects. Whether your job is residential, when your work was actually provided, and whether other deadlines apply are questions only you and your attorney can answer. Texas lien deadlines are strict and missing one permanently destroys the claim. Confirm every date with a Texas construction attorney before you act or decline to act.
