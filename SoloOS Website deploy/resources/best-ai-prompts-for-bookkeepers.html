What are the best AI prompts for bookkeepers?

The best AI prompts for bookkeepers are not prompts that ask AI to make accounting decisions. They are prompts for drafts, checklists, summaries, explanations, and SOPs using fake examples or placeholders instead of private client data.

Safe prompt rule: ask AI to help with language and structure. Keep client names, bank data, payroll records, tax documents, exported ledgers, and exact transaction lists out of the prompt.

1. Missing-items email prompt

Prompt:
Write a friendly email asking a client for missing bookkeeping items for [month]. Use placeholders for [bank statement], [receipt], [deposit explanation], and [transfer question]. Keep it professional, brief, and not scolding.

2. Cleanup checklist prompt

Prompt:
Create a bookkeeping cleanup checklist for a small service business. Include bank feeds, credit cards, duplicate transactions, uncategorized expenses, owner draws, sales platform deposits, payroll review, and final questions. Do not give tax or legal advice.

3. Monthly close summary prompt

Prompt:
Draft a plain-English monthly close summary for a client. Use placeholders for [revenue trend], [expense trend], [open questions], and [next action]. Do not invent numbers. Keep the tone calm and practical.

4. Report explanation prompt

Prompt:
Explain why profit and bank balance are not the same thing for a small business owner. Use simple language, no jargon, and no client-specific data. Include one practical example using fake numbers.

5. SOP draft prompt

Prompt:
Create a first-draft SOP for requesting monthly bookkeeping documents from a client. Include timing, what to request, how to track missing items, and when to follow up. Use placeholders only.

What makes these prompts work

They are specific, they define the task, they include boundaries, and they avoid private data. They also keep the bookkeeper in charge. The output is a draft, not a final professional decision.
